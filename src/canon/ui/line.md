Where the edges of a screen fall. Every line a person can see runs into another
one, or it is a defect: the `align` tool reads the geometry and names the ones
that do not.

- **Only what is painted is a line.** A box with no background and no border is
  invisible, and lining anything up with it is lining up with nothing.
- **A mark with no box is judged by its middle, and text by its baseline.** The
  chevron and the button below it are aligned when their centres agree, however
  differently sized their boxes are.
- **A line a few pixels off another is worse than one standing alone.** Standing
  alone reads as deliberate; nearly aligned reads as broken.
- **One state is half a page.** An accordion open and closed, a menu up and
  down, a tab selected and not: each is captured and judged on its own.
- **Reuse the size before inventing one.** Two controls that will sit above each
  other and cannot share a column are the defect, written earlier.
